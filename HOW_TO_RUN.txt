Hi! Here is how to run the YouTube Downloader on your Windows PC in 1 click.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: EXTRACT THE ZIP FILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click the zip file -> click "Extract All..." -> "Extract".
2. Open the extracted folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: DOUBLE-CLICK "START_DOWNLOADER.bat" (or "START.bat")
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inside the folder, simply double-click:

     ▶  START_DOWNLOADER.bat

- On your first run, it sets up everything automatically in about 1 minute
  (using the Python already installed on your PC).
- When ready, your web browser will automatically open the downloader
  at http://localhost:8501 !
- On all future runs, it starts up in 2 seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: HOW TO DOWNLOAD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy any YouTube video, Shorts, or playlist link.
2. In the app, paste the link into the URL box.
3. Choose "Video" (MP4 up to 4K) or "Audio" (MP3 320kbps).
4. Click "Download Now"!
5. When finished, click "Show file" to open the video in your folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT: FIXING YOUTUBE'S "CONFIRM YOU'RE NOT A BOT" CHECK
------------------------------------------------------------------------
YouTube sometimes blocks automated tools unless you provide your browser cookies.
The app makes this super easy and 100% private:

OPTION A (EASIEST - 1-Click with Chrome or Edge extension):
1. In your browser (Chrome or Edge), install this official free extension:
   Get cookies.txt LOCALLY:
   https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/get-cookiestxt-locally/cclelndahbckbenkjhflpdbgdldlbecc

2. Open YouTube.com (make sure you are logged in).
3. Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar, then click "Export".
   A file named "youtube.com_cookies.txt" will download to your PC.
4. In the app, go to the "Cookie Guide" tab on the left sidebar.
5. Click "Upload cookies.txt Directly" and choose that downloaded file.
   Done! Now all downloads will work seamlessly without any verification errors!

OPTION B (If you use Mozilla Firefox):
1. Just log into YouTube in Firefox.
2. Close Firefox.
3. In app Settings, select "Firefox" under cookies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIVACY NOTE:
All downloads and cookies stay 100% on your local computer. Nothing is sent
to any cloud servers or third parties.
