Hi! Here is how to run the YouTube Downloader on your Windows PC in 1 click.
NO COOKIES OR LOGIN NEEDED FOR REGULAR DOWNLOADS!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: EXTRACT THE ZIP FILE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click the zip file -> click "Extract All..." -> "Extract".
2. Open the extracted folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: DOUBLE-CLICK "START.bat" (or "START_DOWNLOADER.bat")
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inside the folder, simply double-click:

     ▶  START.bat

- On your first run, it sets up everything automatically in about 1 minute.
- When ready, your web browser will automatically open the downloader
  at http://localhost:8501 !
- On all future runs, it starts up in 2 seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: HOW TO DOWNLOAD (ZERO COOKIES NEEDED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy any YouTube video, Shorts, or playlist link.
2. In the app, paste the link into the URL box.
3. Choose "Video" (MP4 up to 4K) or "Audio" (MP3 320 kbps).
4. Click "Download Now"!
5. When finished, click "Locate File" to open the video in your Downloads folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL: ONLY FOR 18+ AGE-RESTRICTED VIDEOS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For 95% of YouTube videos, you do NOT need any cookies at all.
Only if a video is flagged as 18+ age-restricted by YouTube:
1. Open the "Cookie Guide" tab in the left sidebar.
2. Click "Enable Firefox Cookies (1-Click)" OR upload a cookies.txt file.
